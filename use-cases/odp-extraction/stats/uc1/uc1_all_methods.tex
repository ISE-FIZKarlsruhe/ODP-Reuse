\emph{One entity per cluster} scores $P=0.992$ rather than $1.000$ because each ground-truth instance is matched to at most one predicted cluster: where an ontology's instances share entities, only one instance can claim the singleton holding a shared entity, and the remaining instances are matched to singletons lying outside them. Ten GitHub ontologies are affected. The constraint binds every method and not this reference point alone --- 73 of the 442 GitHub and 12 of the 51 Paper \& Repo ontologies have instances that overlap, and on those no hard partition can reach $F_1$ 1. The exact bound averages $0.964$ and $0.976$ over the two portions and falls to $0.333$ at worst.\\
\emph{Populations.} Every method is scored over the same ontologies, the intersection of those both corpora provide, with one exception: steps 2--7 of the full conceptual-components pipeline consume the communities its first stage produces, and on 35 GitHub and 2 Paper \& Repo ontologies that stage produced none, so the pipeline is reported over the 407 and 49 it covers. Scored over those same ontologies its first stage reaches $F_1$ 0.286 and 0.330.\end{flushleft}
